# Implemented now
- Low-poly Three.js Buea streetscape with responsive desktop and mobile camera framing
- Character/test login and server-authoritative fictional FCFA balance and ledger
- Three selectable, persistent story campaigns with 40 authored story beats each:
	- First Light in Buea: a student's arrival, housing, campus life, work and community
	- Market Roots: a local hustler builds a fair, neighbourhood-scale business
	- Mountain Echo: a returning professional organises a community trail project
- Eight chapters per campaign, with five levels per chapter and a clear chapter finale
- Location-specific story activities at Mile 17, Taxi Rank, Molyko, campus, food spots, rooms, the market, Digital Hub and Mount Cameroon
- Story objectives require travel to the named district and completion of its activity
- Difficulty grows from one task per level to two and then three tasks; point and XP rewards rise with level
- Horizontal 40-level progress rail, current mission marker, chapter summary and level-up rewards
- Per-location optional activities that provide small capped points and affect energy, mood, hunger or health
- Three original procedural campaign music themes and activity-specific Web Audio cues, with a mute toggle
- Existing housing, food, university, job, needs, ledger, weather and PWA features
- Responsive desktop/mobile UI with reduced-motion support

# Campaign difficulty
- Levels 1-10: Easy, one required story task
- Levels 11-20: Steady, two required tasks
- Levels 21-30: Hard, two required tasks with wider route jumps
- Levels 31-40: Expert, three required tasks
- Story tasks award `30 + 6 × level` points and `20 + 8 × level` XP when a level is completed.
